# Dr CS Wong design system

A playful KSSM science library for students and teachers. Preserve the supplied classroom reference, illustrated teacher, Comic Sans MS, pastel Forms and existing content.

## Shared conventions

Blue #1667ec is the action colour. Ink #152738 is the foreground. Form colours are blue #dcf2ff, green #e4f7d9, pink #ffe2ec, yellow #fff1bf and lilac #eee1ff. Colour supplements visible Form labels.

Comic Sans MS remains the family. Display lettering is responsive, outlined blue/yellow; section headings 21–30px; simulation titles 17–18px; metadata 12–14px. Headings balance, body text wraps naturally, numeric counts use tabular figures.

Classroom artwork belongs to the entry area. The learning library prioritises readable lists, search, labelled filters and chapter navigation. Form cards have 16px corners, nested buttons 8px, ordinary controls 8–10px. Phosphor duotone supplies every science and external-link icon.

Motion answers actions with 150ms interruptible transitions. Reduced motion removes transforms and smooth scrolling. Keyboard focus uses a visible blue outline; touch controls are at least 44px high.
